# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Beyblade X players — competitive bladers, casual hobbyists, and deck theory-crafters. They use BeyBrew to plan combos, check tournament format legality, and share builds with the community. Primary context: mobile devices at events, or desktop at home.

## Product Purpose

A client-side deck builder for Beyblade X. Users assemble blade/ratchet/bit combos, view aggregated stats, validate against formats (Standard, Limited, custom), and export or share decks via URL or PNG. Success = zero-friction deck assembly with trustworthy format compliance.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, precise, competitive. "Expert confidence."

## Anti-references

Generic toy-brand rainbow clutter. Mobile gacha-game UI density. Slow load times. Paywalls. SaaS-cream minimalism that strips away character.

## Design Principles

- Stats first: attack/defense/stamina/burst must be instantly scannable.
- Speed matters: every interaction feels instantaneous.
- Format fidelity: rules and constraints are visually unambiguous because players trust this for tournament prep.
- Dark by default: reduces eye strain and makes stat glows pop.
- Minimal chrome, maximal data: decoration never competes with part information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch targets ≥ 44px for mobile event use. Color is not the sole means of conveying state.
